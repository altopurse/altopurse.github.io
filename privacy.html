Privacy

Last updated 2 August 2026

This page says exactly what this site records about you, why it is allowed to, how long it is kept and how to get rid of it. It is written to be read, not to be scrolled past.

Who is responsible

JobLeadHub, trading as TUMANIC, is the data controller for this site. Contact for any privacy question or request: tumanicmae@gmail.com.

Visit statistics

This site counts visits. It does not use cookies, does not store anything on your device, and does not build a profile of you. That is why there is no consent banner — there is nothing to consent to.

Your IP address is never written down. To count one person visiting twice as one visitor rather than two, the server makes a one-way hash of your IP address, your browser's user agent, today's date and a secret. The date is inside the hash, so tomorrow the same person produces a completely unrelated value. Nobody, including us, can work backwards from that hash to you, and there is no way to follow anyone from one day to the next.

What is actually kept is a set of daily counts:

  • How many page views and how many visitors
  • Which pages were viewed
  • Which website you arrived from — the site name only, never the full link
  • Whether the device was a phone, tablet or desktop
  • Your browser's preferred language
  • Which sections and buttons were used, as totals

Lawful basis: legitimate interests — understanding whether the site works, using the least information that answers the question.

If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track signal, nothing is counted at all.

Buying something

To post you an artwork we need your name, email address and delivery address. That is kept with the order.

Card details never reach this site. Payment happens on Mollie's own pages, and we are told only whether it succeeded.

Lawful basis: performance of a contract, plus a legal obligation to keep sales records.

The mailing list

If you join the list your email address is stored by our newsletter provider. Every email has an unsubscribe link, and leaving removes you.

Lawful basis: consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

About the Spotify player

Worth being straight about: the embedded Spotify player is Spotify's, not ours, and loading it lets Spotify set its own cookies and see your IP address. We have no control over and no access to that. It is covered by Spotify's own privacy policy. If you would rather it did not load, block third-party cookies or use a content blocker before opening the Music section.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask us to:

  • Tell you what we hold about you, and give you a copy
  • Correct anything wrong
  • Delete it
  • Restrict or object to how it is used
  • Send it to you or someone else in a portable format

On deletion, one honest caveat: if you have bought something, the law requires the sale record to survive for six years. In that case your name, email and address are erased and only the anonymous transaction remains.

Visit statistics cannot be traced to you even by us, so there is nothing there to find, correct or delete on request. That is the point of the design.

Ask by writing to tumanicmae@gmail.com. You will get an answer within one month.
